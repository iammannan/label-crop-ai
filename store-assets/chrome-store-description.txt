## 🚀 Why Sellers Need "Flipkart Label Crop" & "Quick Label Crop"

When you download shipping labels from Flipkart Seller Hub (Seller Dashboard) or Meesho Supplier Hub, the platform gives you an A4 page containing both the shipping label and the tax invoice combined on a single sheet. 

If you own a 4×6 thermal label printer (TVS, TSC, Zebra, Gprinter, Xprinter):
1. The invoice wastes expensive thermal paper rolls.
2. Manually taking screenshots or cropping each page in Photoshop/Acrobat takes hours during big sales (Big Billion Days, Festive Sales).
3. Paid desktop software charges monthly subscriptions and slows down your PC.
4. Cloud-based croppers upload your confidential customer addresses and order details to remote servers.

LabelCrop AI solves every single one of these problems with our automated Flipkart label crop and quick label crop engine!

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: How does Flipkart label crop work with this extension?**
A: When you download your batch dispatch from Flipkart Seller Hub, upload the PDF to LabelCrop AI. Our tool automatically detects the Flipkart layout, trims away the tax invoice section, aligns the shipping barcode and address, and outputs a clean 4×6 inch PDF ready for your thermal printer.

**Q: Is quick label crop free to use?**
A: Yes! LabelCrop AI is 100% free with no monthly subscription, no hidden charges, and no watermark on your labels.

**Q: Will the Ekart or Delhivery barcodes remain scannable?**
A: Absolutely. LabelCrop AI maintains original vector fidelity and DPI resolution without compression artifacts, ensuring 100% first-pass scan rates by delivery executives.

**Q: Does my customer data get uploaded to any server?**
A: Never. All label parsing, cropping, and rendering run locally on your machine inside your Chrome browser. No PDF or customer address is ever transmitted across the internet.

**Q: Can I merge multiple PDF files from different dispatches?**
A: Yes! Use our Bulk PDF Merge feature to combine dispatches from multiple Flipkart accounts or different marketplaces into one continuous thermal print roll.
