## Pro-Tips for Thermal Printer Owners (TVS, TSC, Zebra, Gprinter)

To ensure zero delivery pickup rejections:

1. **Set Print Margins to "None":** When the print dialog opens in Chrome, set margins to *None* and scale to *Fit to Printable Area* or *100%*.
2. **Use High-Contrast Thermal Rolls:** Cheap rolls fade under heat. Always use water-resistant, scratch-proof thermal rolls.
3. **Organize by Courier Before Packing:** Use a courier-wise sorting feature to group your Ekart, Delhivery, Shadowfax, and Xpressbees packages separately before the courier pickup van arrives.
