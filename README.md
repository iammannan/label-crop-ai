## ⚡ Why Use Label Crop AI?

Online marketplace seller panels (Meesho Supplier Panel, Flipkart Seller Hub, Amazon India Seller Central) bundle shipping labels and tax invoices together on standard A4 PDF pages. 

Printing these raw pages directly on 4-inch direct thermal barcode printers causes major dispatch issues:
1. **Shrunken, Unscannable Barcodes**: The printer driver scales the entire A4 page down onto one 4×6 sticker, producing tiny barcodes that logistics scanners (Valmo, Ekart, Delhivery, Shadowfax) cannot read.
2. **Expensive Paper Waste**: Up to 50% of costly thermal sticker rolls are wasted printing full tax invoices that should be filed digitally or printed on ordinary paper.
3. **Manual Handover Chaos**: Hundreds of mixed parcels have to be sorted manually by courier partner when pickup riders arrive.

**Label Crop AI** solves this instantly by processing multi-page PDFs directly inside your browser or mobile phone with zero cloud upload.

## 📖 How to Use

1. **Open the Tool**: Visit **[labelcropai.web.app](https://labelcropai.web.app)** on your browser, install the **[Chrome Extension](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/labelcrop-ai-shipping-lab/mmblcneiohcnglkfclhbkgkbeocejcnk)**, or download the **[Android App](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=ecom.label.crop.tool&hl=en)**.

3. **Select Layout**: Choose **4×6 Thermal** or **A4 Sticker Sheet (2/4/6/8-up)**.
4. **Download & Print**: Click **Crop Labels** — your print-ready PDF is generated immediately without waiting.
